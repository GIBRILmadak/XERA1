## Canonical description

XERA1 is a Proof of Building platform that helps developers, students, designers, entrepreneurs, and technology creators document what they build, track their progress, and build a reputation based on their actual work and achievements.

XERA1 is designed to make technical work visible, structured, and discoverable.

The platform focuses on evidence of execution rather than traditional claims, CV-only profiles, or self-reported skills.

## Core platform concepts

### Profile

A XERA1 profile represents a builder and their documented work, projects, progress, achievements, and reputation.

### Arc

An Arc represents a structured progression around a project or objective.

Arcs can contain:
- A title
- A description
- Milestones
- Progress
- Evidence
- Technical activity
- Project-related updates

Arcs are designed to make progression easier to follow and understand.

### Trace

A Trace is a progress update documenting activity, work, learning, experimentation, or advancement on a project.

### Proof

Proof provides evidence associated with a project, action, milestone, or achievement.

Proof can include relevant media, descriptions, project evidence, or other admissible evidence depending on the context.

### Milestone

A milestone represents a significant step in the progression of a project or Arc.

### Builder reputation

XERA1 enables builders to develop a reputation based on documented work and achievements over time.

### Professional pages

XERA1 provides professional pages designed for professional identities, organizations, companies, and projects.

Professional pages can provide additional visibility and professional features.

### Verification

XERA1 provides verification mechanisms and badges that can indicate specific statuses, achievements, or participation.

Verification status should not be interpreted as a universal guarantee of every claim made by a user.
